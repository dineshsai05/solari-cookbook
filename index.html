Permit BL2024-1706, Village of Pinecrest, Florida

A Solari Browser searched the eTRAKiT portal, read 17 departmental reviews and the comment page behind each, listed 23 attachments with the portal's VOID labels, and downloaded the applicant response and both structural revisions inside the session. A Solari Sandbox extracted the text. The report shows change detection against the previous capture, a replay as of March 25, 2025, and an AI-linked checklist with every citation checked.
