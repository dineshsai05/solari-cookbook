PermitPilot · Solari cookbook application · live proof

Three real permit cases, read by an agent

Sanitized reports from live Solari runs on September 29, 2026. Every quote links back to the public source it came from. Nothing was submitted or changed on any portal.

Permit BL2024-1706, Village of Pinecrest, Florida

A Solari Browser searched the eTRAKiT portal, read 17 departmental reviews and the comment page behind each, listed 23 attachments with the portal's VOID labels, and downloaded the applicant response and both structural revisions inside the session. A Solari Sandbox extracted the text. A Solari Desktop opened the tracker in LibreOffice Calc. The report shows change detection against the previous capture, a replay as of March 25, 2025, and an AI-linked checklist with every citation checked.

Permit BP26-00421, Town of Atherton, California

The same adapter on a different eTRAKiT deployment: an open grading and drainage permit with 11 review rows, two still pending. The town publishes no reviewer notes or attachments, so the sandbox and the checklist are skipped and the run is pure status monitoring. The second capture reported no change against the first; a real change will show as a diff.

Farmdale Apartments, Woodburn, Oregon
