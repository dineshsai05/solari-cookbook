Farmdale Apartments

1219 & 1233 W Lincoln Street, Woodburn, Oregon
DR 25-02 · PAR 24-03 · VAR 25-03 · MOC 25-02

Farmdale is a proposed 45-unit apartment redevelopment in Woodburn. This review follows its modification-of-conditions case: the applicant sought to remove a pedestrian corridor requirement and restrict vehicle access between two parcels. The December 11, 2025 decision allowed restricted vehicle access, denied removal of the corridor requirement, and allowed its relocation. Other original conditions remained unchanged. This is land-use review history, not confirmation of a building permit or completed construction.
